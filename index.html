About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Iowa. My research spans interpretable representation learning for glaucoma OCT imaging, longitudinal disease progression modeling, clinical risk prediction using electronic health records, and scientific computing.

I am interested in Data Science, Machine Learning, and Applied AI opportunities that connect quantitative modeling with real-world problems.
